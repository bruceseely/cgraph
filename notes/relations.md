## Relations

A relation defines how concepts are related to each other. The semantics of the relation is determined by its relation-type. There is a direction implied by the arcs connecting the relation to concepts. Relations have a single output and, as currently implemented, a single output. It is possible include relations with multiple inputs. Sowa likes to use the example of 'between' to indicate that conncept designated by the output arc is between the concepts designated by the two input arcs.

For information on relation-types see the Types section.
