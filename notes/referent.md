## Referent

A referent field of a concept can be NIL, meaning the concept is a generic concept, or a referent object containing one of the following:

- an individual
- a set: (a collection of individual)
- a context, containing  a graph

The concept-type of the containing concept determines whether the referent represents a context.

The purpose of the referent is to adapt to the actual object used as a referent.

Currently, everything is handled as an Individual object.
